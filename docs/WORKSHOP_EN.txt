[h1]China Railway FXN5C Diesel Locomotive[/h1]
China Railway blue, Jinwen Railway and a separate red FXN5C 0001 prototype for Transport Fever 2: eleven numbered locomotives.

[h2]v0.44 update[/h2]
The red 0001 has its own cab, four upper headlights, vertically paired lower lamps, revised doors/ladders and raised metallic lettering. This update fixes the cab-corner transition planes, continuous raked window profile, black cab divider and vertical front handrails. Production locomotives gain refined roof panels, exhaust/grille divisions, recessed rectangular windscreens, covered horns and thicker sandboxes. Fuxing lettering uses a lower-right shadow; CR/prototype waist stripes are warmer orange-yellow.
All purchase icons now show a horizontal whole-locomotive side view with a simple track display. The missing prototype TGA paths are supplied: 52 images cover all numbered models and the two production groups, at two UI sizes and standard/high density.
Fans rotate; large louvers remain open at 55 degrees; smaller louvers use a 0.8-second top-to-bottom wave, up to 40 degrees, with nine larger blades per window at near LOD. All-animation, fans-only and static options remain available. Reload after changing options. These are display settings, not thermal simulation.

[h2]Contents[/h2]
China Railway: 0051, 0066 (Shanghai); 0096, 0102 (Nanjing East); 0057, 0081 (Hangzhou); 0035, 0115 (Xuzhou).
Jinwen Railway: 7005, 7006.
Red prototype: 0001, a separate purchase entry outside the two production groups. Search for 0001 or Red Prototype.

[h2]In-game configuration[/h2]
Power: 3530 kW. Tractive effort: 580 kN. Top speed: 120 km/h. Weight: 150 t.
All years by default; optionally keep CR 2024/Jinwen 2025 availability. The prototype currently has no year restriction and temporarily reuses production performance. Depot markings, dates and parameters are project configurations, not certified vehicle histories.

[h2]Validation and limitations[/h2]
Native/static, source-consistency, local geometry and icon checks passed. Systematic in-game/Model Editor testing is pending; prototype purchase visibility needs confirmation after a full game restart. Project previews are offline renders.
This is a reference-based reconstruction, not factory CAD. Bogies, coupling compatibility and cab interiors remain approximate. Linkage rods use deforming skinning; prototype access ladders may interfere at larger steering angles. Extreme curves, hidden structures and directional lights need further game testing. Per-number leading inner-lamp colors are custom configurations.
